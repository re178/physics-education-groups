How it works

  1. Enter your details. Your Registration Number is your unique identifier — it cannot be reused across groups.
  2. Group matching. Group names are matched case-insensitively and whitespace-insensitively, so “Group A”, “group a”, and “ GROUP A ” are the same group.
  3. Group Leader. The first member to register in a new group automatically becomes the Group Leader.
  4. Capacity. Each group holds a limited number of members. If your chosen group is full, you will be asked to pick another group or contact the Administrator.
  5. Lab Manuals. Once your registration is accepted, your Lab Manual is available for collection. Show the time and day you paid (or your M-Pesa code) to receive it.
  6. Login later. After registering, log in with your Group Name (username) and Registration Number (password) to view your group.
