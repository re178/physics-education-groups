How to Submit Your Registration

Follow these simple steps. Read carefully.

Use this guide to register into your Internet Computing group.

STEP 1 — Fill in your details

Enter these four things:

  • Your Registration Number (e.g. REG12345)
  • Your Full Name
  • Your Phone Number (e.g. 0712345678)
  • The Group you want to join — pick one from the dropdown

The dropdown shows every group that still has room. If a group is already full, it will appear greyed out and you will not be able to select it.

STEP 2 — Submit your registration

Click SUBMIT REGISTRATION. You will see a green success message. You are done.

STEP 3 — Logging in later

After registering, log in at the Member Login page by selecting the same group from the dropdown and entering your Registration Number as the password.

Each phone can register only once. Do not share your registration details with anyone.

How Groups Work

Read this before choosing a group.

Group list

Groups are pre-created by the Administrator and appear in the dropdown as Group 1, Group 2, and so on. Pick whichever group you have agreed on with your team.

Capacity

Each group has a fixed capacity. Once a group is full, it can no longer be selected — pick a different group that still has room.

Group Leader

The first person to register in a group automatically becomes that group's Group Leader.

Key rules

  • One phone = one registration
  • One registration number = one student
  • Every member of a team must register into the SAME group
  • Full groups cannot be chosen
  • Group leaders are assigned automatically
