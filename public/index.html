How to Submit Your Registration

Follow these simple steps. Read carefully.

Use this guide to register your Physics Education group and collect your Lab Manual.

STEP 1 — Fill in your details

Enter these four things:

  • Your Registration Number (e.g. REG12345)
  • Your Full Name
  • Your Phone Number (e.g. 0712345678)
  • The Group Name you want to join

STEP 2 — If you see a Payment box

When payment is required, send KSH 45 via M-Pesa to 0741742291 (Pochi la Biashara).

⚠️ The moment you pay, write down THREE things:

  • 🕐 The time you paid (e.g. 3:45 PM)
  • 📅 The day you paid (e.g. Monday, 23 Sept)
  • 🔢 The M-Pesa code from your SMS

Then paste the M-Pesa code and the time & day into the form.

STEP 3 — Submit your registration

Click SUBMIT REGISTRATION. You will see a green success message. You are done.

STEP 4 — Collect your Lab Manual

Show the Administrator the time and day you paid or your M-Pesa code. That is how you receive your Lab Manual.

Logging in later

After registering, log in at the Member Login page using your Group Name as the username and your Registration Number as the password.

Do not share your M-Pesa SMS or code with anyone. Each phone can register only once.

Group Formation Rules

Read this before choosing a group name.

Group size

  • Minimum: 13 people
  • Maximum: 15 people

Agree on ONE group name — and why it matters

Everyone in the group must type the exact same group name. If different people type different names, the system will create separate groups and your team will be split up.

✓ Everyone types: Physics Practical Group A

✗ Avoid: Group A, group a, GroupA — any other difference puts you in a different group.

Rule: Sit together, agree on the spelling, and everyone uses the same name.

Key rules

  • One phone = one registration
  • One registration number = one student
  • Group maximum = 15 members
  • Group minimum = 13 members
  • Everyone must use the SAME group name
